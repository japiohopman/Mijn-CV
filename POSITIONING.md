# 🎯 Portfolio Positioning Framework & Core Messaging Strategy

This document defines the core professional positioning, narrative architecture, tone of voice, and messaging hierarchy for Jaap Hopman's portfolio. It serves as the single source of truth for all portfolio copy, section structures, and communications.

---

## 1. Core Positioning Statement & Primary Value Proposition

### Title & Core Professional Identity
**Creative Developer & Technologist**

### Concise Positioning Statement
> **Jaap Hopman is a Creative Developer & Technologist based in Amsterdam who builds interactive web applications, games, and AI-driven tools using TypeScript, React, Node.js, and modern frameworks. Combining a background in professional kitchens with creative media discipline, he translates complex ideas into functional, resilient software with a hands-on execution mindset.**

### Primary Value Proposition
- **Hands-On Execution**: Focus on shipping working software rather than abstract concepts or generic templates.
- **Systemic & Practical Problem Solving**: Culinary leadership and kitchen management instil high-pressure discipline, rigorous preparation, speed, and accountability.
- **Creative Technology Integration**: Bridging front-end engineering, game logic (Phaser/Godot), and practical AI workflows (LLM APIs, local models, audio/speech processing).

## 4. Tone of Voice Guidelines

- **Authentic & Grounded:** Direct, clear language without inflated buzzwords or generic corporate jargon.
- **Confident & Practical:** Focus on what is built, tested, and working ("leren door te bouwen").
- **Clear & Human:** Easy to read for both technical engineering leads and non-technical recruiters.
- **Honest:** No invented claims, fake metrics, or exaggerated project achievements.
